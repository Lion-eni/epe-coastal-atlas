# Data Sources and Provenance

## 1. Administrative Boundaries

**Source:** GRID3 Nigeria Local Government Area Boundaries  
**Purpose:** Identify and define the Epe Local Government Area study boundary.  
**Status:** Candidate source identified; local file path and geometry must be verified before analysis.

## 2. Waterways and Waterbodies

**Source:** ArcGIS feature services containing mapped waterways and waterbodies in the Epe area.  
**Purpose:** Provide contextual hydrographic information for the study area.  
**Status:** Candidate datasets identified; coordinate reference systems, geometry validity, and local file availability require verification.

## 3. Fisheries and Aquaculture Locations

**Sources:** OpenStreetMap, published research, government materials, and secondary sources.  
**Purpose:** Build a candidate inventory of fish markets, landing sites, jetties, and aquaculture facilities.  
**Status:** Initial inventory compiled. Coordinates and evidence quality require further review. Candidate records are not all independently verified.

## 4. Satellite Data

**Potential source:** Sentinel-2 imagery.  
**Purpose:** Investigate suitable land-cover, vegetation, water-related, and environmental change indicators.  
**Status:** Planned; imagery has not yet been processed.

## 5. Fishing Activity Data

**Potential source:** Global Fishing Watch.  
**Purpose:** Assess whether available vessel activity data can contribute to the study.  
**Limitation:** AIS-based activity data do not provide a complete inventory of small-scale artisanal fishing activity.  
**Status:** Planned; data availability and access requirements have not yet been confirmed.

## Data Quality Principles

- Record source, acquisition date, coordinate reference system, and processing steps.
- Distinguish mapped locations from independently verified locations.
- Document uncertainty and known limitations.
- Do not treat candidate locations as confirmed field observations.
- Do not infer pollution directly from satellite imagery without suitable evidence and validation.
